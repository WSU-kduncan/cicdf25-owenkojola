Why Cats Are Awesome

Cats are curious, independent, and surprisingly affectionate. They spend a lot of time napping, exploring, and watching the world from windowsills.

Fun Cat Facts

  • Cats sleep 12–16 hours per day.
  • They can make over 100 different vocal sounds.
  • A group of kittens is called a litter.
